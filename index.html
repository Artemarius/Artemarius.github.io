Visual-inertial odometry · v1.0.0

pocketvio

Real-time visual-inertial odometry on Android: a multi-state constraint Kalman filter (MSCKF) in C++20 with a Vulkan compute front-end, running live on the phone. IMU-first, from raw 475 Hz streams with noise from Allan variance and an on-device calibration wizard. Deterministic: the phone records its own input, and its desktop replay is bit-identical run to run.

Where it loses: on walked room loops ARCore ends 0.004 m from the tags against pocketvio's 0.116 m. ARCore relocalises; pocketvio is odometry only, with no loop closure. All errors are measured against AprilTags seen by the phone's own camera. Measured on the Galaxy S24 Ultra and S25 Ultra.

3D Gaussian Splatting · v0.3.0

PocketSplat

A native Vulkan 1.3 renderer for 3D Gaussian Splatting on Android, written from the papers for mobile GPUs: chunk cull, a compacting preprocess, an own radix sort with no inter-workgroup waits, and one indirect draw. A thermal controller trades image quality for heat. Measured like-for-like against the renderers people use: same phone, same splats, same pixels.

Where it loses: at equal work on the S25 Ultra, PocketSplat still spends 9 % more energy per frame than SuperSplat on stump, and splatkit's sort is about 1.6× cheaper per key. Two Adreno phones measured; Mali and Xclipse are untested. Slower experiments are kept as written-up negative results.
